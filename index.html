    1. Introduction: Biticius Farticus and the Farticus Power

    Biticius Farticus (BTF) is not just another meme token. It is a token with real and exclusive utility within the Runes Protocol ecosystem. Biticius has the unique mystical ability known as "Farticus Power" to transform methane sats into Bitcoin BTC, with the help of the Wizard of Magic Money.

    This narrative has led to the creation of a highly recognizable and symbolic inscription: Bitcoin BTC. When users search for "Bitcoin" on major platforms like CoinMarketCap or CoinGecko, the term "Bitcoin BTC" appears, creating an immediate mental link to one of the most sought-after digital assets in the world.

    This whitepaper presents the philosophical foundations, economic structure, and mechanisms that empower BTF and Bitcoin BTC as unique, narrative-backed digital assets with real-world impact.

    4. Deflationary Model & Price Progression Lock Mechanism

    BITCOIN•BTC (ᛒ) will be released progressively using a deflationary model that ties each batch to market activity and completion.

    • No batch is released until the previous one is completely sold.
    • Next price level is set at either:
    • 25% higher than the last batch price, or
    • 25% higher than the current market price on Unisat
      ➤ whichever is higher.

    This ensures: - Upward price pressure. - Incentivized early participation. - A deflationary and demand-driven supply model.

    5. Use of Funds

    All funds collected from BITCOIN•BTC (ᛒ) sales will be allocated strategically for:

    • CEX Listings: Progressively across Tier 3, Tier 2, and Tier 1 platforms.
    • Brand Visibility & Demand Growth: Enhancing recognition of Biticius Farticus (BTF) and BITCOIN•BTC (ᛒ).

    This approach supports the transition from centralized management to decentralized community governance, ensuring:

    • Sustainable development.
    • Efficient capital allocation.
    • Growth of the Biticius ecosystem.

    The limited use of minting (only 10%) avoids unnecessary inflation and miner-dilution, ensuring that resources are directed where they matter most: community and infrastructure.

    7. Purchase Process

    1. Holder sends native Bitcoin sats to the official BITCOIN•BTC token wallet (verifiable on OKX Explorer or Unisat).
    2. Team verifies the sender's BTF balance.
    3. Equivalent BTC is sent, minus fees.

    This manual process ensures transparency and reward for real holders.

    🔓 First Batch Release Notice

    The first batch of Bitcoin BTC has already been released on Unisat for open public sale at a fixed price of 100 sats per Bitcoin BTC.

    This gives the community two options for acquiring Bitcoin BTC:

    1. Open Market Purchase: Buy directly from Unisat at the current listed price (starting at 100 sats per BTC token).
    2. Discounted Purchase via BTF Holding: Follow the discounted purchase model described above by holding Biticius Farticus (BTF) and accessing exclusive batch pricing benefits through the official wallet process.

    This dual model ensures maximum accessibility while preserving the core deflationary mechanics and community-based incentives.
